SIOVS STORE, INVENTORY & ASSET   MANAGEMENT SYSTEMFinal Functional Requirements / Module Structure1. PurposeThe proposed system shall provide a centralized computerized system for management and monitoring of:Purchase-to-Store receipts Main Store inventory Medicines and surgical consumables Departmental stock OPD, IPD, OT and Emergency issues/consumption Patient-wise consumption Surgery-wise consumption Batch and expiry management Stock verification Fixed assets Dead stock Asset transfer Repair & maintenance Warranty/AMC Condemnation/disposal Manual register reconciliation Audit trail and management reporting The system shall initially commence from the Purchase Section's purchased material being delivered to and received by the SIOVS Store. The complete procurement/tender process may be incorporated subsequently.2. Basic Transaction FlowThe primary inventory flow shall be:Purchase Section↓Purchase Order↓Supplier Delivery / Delivery Challan↓SIOVS Store↓Inspection & Verification↓GRN / Goods Received Note↓Main Store Stock↓Digital Departmental Requisition↓Issue to OPD / IPD / OT / Emergency↓Departmental Stock↓Patient-wise / Surgery-wise Consumption↓Return / Adjustment / Stock VerificationThis will ensure that stock movement can be traced from purchase through final consumption.3. Purchase Section ModuleInitially, the Purchase Section will record only those purchase details necessary for Store receiving and reconciliation.Purchase Order informationPO No. PO Date Supplier Purchase reference Tender/quotation reference Item Item code Quantity ordered Unit Rate Amount Taxes Total value Expected delivery date Warranty, where applicable Supporting documents The Purchase Section shall not directly increase Store stock.Stock will be increased only after the Store receives the material and generates the GRN.4. Goods Received Note (GRN)The Store shall create a GRN against the relevant Purchase Order and Delivery Challan.GRN fieldsGRN No. — system generated PO No. Supplier Delivery Challan No. Delivery Challan Date Date received Item code Item description Unit Quantity ordered Quantity received Quantity accepted Quantity rejected Batch/Lot No. Manufacturing date Expiry date Rate Total value Inspection/verification status Remarks Attached Delivery Challan Invoice Inspection report Only approved/accepted GRN quantities shall be posted to available Store stock.5. Item MasterEvery item shall have a unique item code.Categories may include:Medicines Ophthalmic medicines Surgical consumables General medical consumables Laboratory consumables Cleaning materials Stationery Electrical items Engineering materials Other consumable items The Item Master should contain:Item Code Item Name Category Sub-category Unit Minimum level Maximum level Reorder level Average consumption Storage location Applicable batch/expiry requirement Active/inactive status 6. Main Store InventoryThe system shall automatically maintain the stock ledger:Opening Stock + Receipts + Returns – Issues – Consumption/Adjustments = Closing StockThe Store shall be able to view:Current balance Available quantity Reserved quantity Batch-wise quantity Expiry Rate Stock value Location/bin Previous transactions 7. Batch and Expiry ManagementFor medicines, ophthalmic products and applicable consumables, the system shall maintain:Batch number Manufacturing date Expiry date Quantity Supplier GRN Unit cost Location Automatic alertsExpired Expiring within 30 days Expiring within 60 days Expiring within 90 days Expiring within 180 days The system should support FEFO (First Expiry, First Out) wherever applicable.8. Digital Departmental RequisitionAuthorized departments shall be able to initiate digital requirements.DepartmentsOPD Pharmacy IPD/Wards OT Emergency Other authorized sections WorkflowDepartment → Requisition → Authorization → Store Verification → IssueThe requisition shall contain:Requisition No. Department Date Item Required quantity Approved quantity Issued quantity Balance Purpose Requesting officer Approving officer Store officer 9. Departmental StockThe system shall maintain separate virtual stock for each department.For example:Main Store: 10,000 syringesIssue to OPD:Main Store: 9,000OPD: 1,000After OPD consumption:OPD: 500This will allow complete tracing of material.10. OPD Patient-wise ConsumptionConsidering the daily OPD volume of approximately 1,500–2,000 patients, the system should be designed for high-volume transaction processing.Where SIOVS HMIS/patient registration data is available, patient information should preferably flow electronically rather than requiring duplicate manual entry.RecordMR/Patient No./Name/CNIC (If applicable) Visit date Department Item Batch Quantity User Date/time The system should be able to produce:Patient → Treatment/Service → Item → Batch → Quantity consumed11. OT / Surgery-wise ConsumptionConsidering 30+ surgeries daily, OT shall have a dedicated consumption module.Surgery informationSurgery No. MR No. Patient Date Surgeon Procedure OT Case type ConsumptionIOL Surgical gloves Syringes Cannulas Sutures Viscoelastic Medicines Surgical packs Other consumables At completion of the surgery, the authorized consumption shall be posted to the system.The system should therefore answer:What material was consumed for a particular surgery/patient?andWhat was the total consumption of a particular procedure during a specified period?12. IPD/Ward ConsumptionFor admitted patients:MR No. → Admission → Ward/Bed → Treatment → Medicine/Consumable → ConsumptionThe system should maintain:Patient Ward Bed Admission date Discharge date Doctor Item Batch Quantity Date/time User 13. Emergency ConsumptionEmergency shall have a separate rapid-consumption facility.The system should allow authorized Emergency staff to record:Patient/MR No. Item Quantity Batch Date/time User Purpose Where immediate consumption makes prior requisition impractical, the system may allow post-consumption regularization/authorization, with a complete audit trail.14. ReturnsThe system shall allow departments to return unused material.Example:Issued: 100Consumed: 70Returned: 20Department balance: 10Returned stock should be subject to verification before being added to available stock.15. Stock AdjustmentControlled adjustments shall be available for:Damage Expiry Breakage Shortage Excess Correction Other authorized reasons Users shall not be permitted to simply overwrite stock balances.Every adjustment must have:Reason Quantity Value Supporting document Requesting user Authorizing officer Date/time Audit trail 16. Physical Stock VerificationThe system shall support periodic physical stocktaking.ReportItemSystem QtyPhysical QtyDifferenceItem A5,0004,980-20Item B3,0003,0000The system shall record:Stocktaking date Committee/members Book balance Physical balance Difference Explanation Adjustment Approval Remarks 17. Minimum / Maximum / Reorder LevelEach important item may have:Minimum stock Maximum stock Reorder level Average consumption Lead time Dashboard alerts:Critical StockBelow Reorder LevelAdequate StockThis will help prevent stock-outs of essential medicines and surgical consumables.18. Fixed Asset RegisterFixed assets shall be maintained separately from consumable inventory.Asset informationAsset ID Category Description Make Model Serial number PO Invoice GRN Purchase date Cost Department Location Custodian Warranty AMC Useful life Status Disposal date Disposal reference Each asset should receive a unique SIOVS Asset ID.19. Dead Stock RegisterA separate computerized Dead Stock Register shall be maintained for non-consumable/durable items as applicable.Examples:Furniture Computers Printers Air conditioners Medical equipment Electrical equipment Office equipment Other durable items The system should clearly distinguish:Consumable Stock | Fixed Assets | Dead Stock20. Asset TransferAsset location/custodian should not simply be overwritten.An Asset Transfer Transaction shall record:Asset ID Previous location New location Previous custodian New custodian Transfer date Reason Authorized by Received by Transfer reference Complete transfer history shall remain available.21. Repair & MaintenanceEvery asset should be capable of being linked with its maintenance history.Maintenance RequestMaintenance No. Asset ID Department Problem Date reported Reported by Priority Technician/vendor Inspection Estimated cost Approval Repair details Parts replaced Labour cost Total cost Date returned Warranty Remarks 22. Preventive MaintenanceThe system shall maintain preventive maintenance schedules for important assets.For example:AssetMaintenanceFrequencyNext DueOT MicroscopeServicing6 MonthsJan-27GeneratorServiceMonthlyOct-26ACServiceQuarterlyDec-26Automatic due-date alerts should be generated.23. Warranty & AMCThe system should provide:Warranty start date Warranty expiry AMC start date AMC expiry Vendor Contract/reference Service dates Next service Cost Alerts should be generated before expiry.24. Condemnation / DisposalFor obsolete, damaged or unserviceable assets:Asset → Inspection → Condemnation → Competent Approval → Disposal → ClosureThe system shall retain the complete historical record.The asset should not disappear from the database; its status should change to:Condemned / Disposed25. Manual RegistersSince SIOVS is a government institution and manual registers are presently maintained, the software shall initially operate alongside the existing manual record system.The software shall therefore include a:Manual Register ReferenceRegister Name Page No. Entry No. Date Section Remarks Example:Store Issue Register – Page 125 – Entry No. 348This reference can be attached to the corresponding electronic transaction.26. Manual vs Digital ReconciliationA dedicated reconciliation facility shall be provided to compare:Manual Register ↔ Software RecordThe system should record:Register checked Page Entry Software transaction Difference Reason Corrective action Verified by Date This will be particularly useful for Internal Audit and physical verification.27. Deletion Authorization — DD FinanceThis shall be a mandatory control.Ordinary users shall not have unrestricted authority to delete transactions.Proposed workflowUser creates entry↓Entry is editable while in draft↓Deletion Request↓DD Finance — Incharge of Section↓Review↓Approve / Reject↓Deleted/CancelledDD Finance shall be authorized to:View deletion requests Review original entry Review supporting documents Review reason Approve deletion Reject deletion Return request for clarification The deletion authorization should specifically be assigned to:DD Finance, being Incharge of the concerned section, or such officer as may subsequently be formally authorized by the competent authority.28. Deletion Audit TrailEven after deletion from the active transaction list, the system shall preserve an immutable audit record containing:Original transaction number Original date/time Section Description Quantity Value Original user Reason for deletion Deletion request date/time DD Finance authorization Authorizing officer Authorization date/time Remarks Supporting documents/reference The system should therefore use three statuses:ACTIVE → DELETION REQUESTED → DELETED/CANCELLEDThis prevents unauthorized disappearance of official records.Stock transactionsWhere a deleted transaction affects stock, the system must automatically reverse the corresponding stock movement rather than merely removing the screen entry.29. Implementation PhasesPhase-I — Store & InventoryThe initial implementation should include:Item Master Supplier Master Purchase Order information Delivery Challan GRN Main Store Batch/expiry Digital requisition Issue Departmental stock OPD consumption IPD consumption OT/surgery consumption Emergency consumption Returns Stock adjustment Physical verification Manual register references Manual/digital reconciliation User roles DD Finance deletion authorization Audit trail Store reports Phase-II — AssetsFixed Asset Register Dead Stock Register Asset transfer Repair & maintenance Preventive maintenance Warranty AMC Condemnation/disposal Asset reports Phase-III — Complete ProcurementAt a later stage, the system may be expanded to include:Procurement planning Annual procurement plan Tender management SPPRA-related processes Quotations Comparative statements Bid evaluation Approvals Purchase orders Supplier performance Contract management oThis approach avoids making the first version unnecessarily complicated while ensuring that the architecture can accommodate the complete procurement cycle later.34. Final Recommended Principle for SIOVSThe software should be designed around traceability and accountability, rather than merely computerized registers.Every important item should be traceable as follows:Where purchased → From whom purchased → When received → GRN → Batch → Main Store → Which department received it → Which patient/surgery consumed it → What quantity remained → Whether it was returned/adjusted → Who entered it → Who approved it → Whether any subsequent deletion/cancellation occurred.For assets:Purchase → Receipt → Asset ID → Location → Custodian → Transfer → Repair →
